Zoomie Zoe privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

In short: Zoomie Zoe collects no personal data. The website counts visits and app installs anonymously, with no cookies. Your best score and two small settings are stored only on your own device.

What we collect

No personal data. Zoomie Zoe has no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies.

The website version (vinayavc.github.io/zoomie-zoe) counts visits and app installs anonymously with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly, open-source counter. Each time the page loads, it sends one hit with the page address, the page title and the website you came from (if any). It also sends one hit when you install the web app and one each time you open the installed app. Nothing about your games, scores or settings is sent.

No cookies are set and no ID is stored on your device. GoatCounter receives your IP address and browser type with each request, like any website does, but it doesn't store them; it keeps only anonymous totals. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted at all. If you're offline, nothing is sent.

The Chrome extension never sends anything over the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves three small values so they're still there next time you play:

  • your best score (a number),
  • whether sound is on or off, and
  • whether you've already seen the how-to-play card.

The Chrome extension keeps them in Chrome's local extension storage. The web version keeps them in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are never shared with us or anyone else. Removing the extension, or clearing your browser's site data, deletes them.

Permissions

The Chrome extension asks for one permission, storage. It uses it only to save the three values above.

Children

Zoomie Zoe is safe for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above before the new version is released.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the game's GitHub page, or email the address shown on the Zoomie Zoe Chrome Web Store listing.
