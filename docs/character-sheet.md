# Character Sheet: Zoe

The star of Doxie Arcade, based on Vinny's own dog. Every agent works from this sheet.
Status: **approved by Vinny (Oct 2, 2026).** The look and palette were updated from the reference photos the same day.

## Identity
- **Name on screen:** Zoe
- **Nicknames:** none given yet
- **Breed:** miniature long-haired dachshund

## Look
- **Coat:** blonde to light apricot, long and silky. It is **two-tone**: a warmer golden-apricot saddle along her back and the top of her head, fading to pale cream on her ears, chest, belly, paws, and leg feathering.
- **Eyes:** **both warm dark brown.** Vinny's call (Oct 2, 2026): one eye looks blue in the photos, but the game draws both brown. Make them big, soft, and expressive, with a white highlight.
- **Nose:** rosy liver brown, **not black**
- **Ears:** big, long, and feathery. They are much paler than her body, nearly cream, with wavy fringe that hangs below her jaw. They are her signature shape and should bounce and trail as she moves.
- **Tail:** a feathered plume, often carried up in a soft curve
- **Face:** slim muzzle, soft brows, and a gentle, slightly worried, attentive expression
- **Accessories:** a dark navy collar with a small gold tag
- **Size:** mini, with a compact head and short legs, and a long body that is the core game mechanic
- **Palette (watercolor, eyeballed from photos):**
  - Coat: cream highlight `#F6EAD5`, pale cream (ears, chest) `#EBD9BC`, golden-apricot body `#D9AA6E`, saddle and shadow `#BF8C55`
  - Face: nose `#8C5B52`, eyes `#2E211A`
  - Collar `#2E3646`, gold tag `#C9A84C`
- **Her world (from the photos):** a blue-grey sherpa pillow and blanket (`#7E8C9C`), an indigo and dusty-pink floral bedspread, sunlit windowsills she watches from, and soft linen sofas. The cool blue-greys contrast nicely with her warm cream coat, and **the blankets are her real burrowing spot.**
- **Reference photos:** `docs/ref/Photos-1-001/` (4 shots: curled on the bed, close-up face, sitting with tail up, standing at the window). Use them for **color and proportion only**. The drawn Zoe is a stylized watercolor and paper-cutout interpretation, not a trace.

## Personality
- **Loves:** her **ball** (favorite toy), **cheese** (top snack), and **burrowing into blankets**
- **Habits:** **zoomies**, **stealing things**, and **sleeping in odd poses**
- **Dislikes:** **other dogs** and **baths**

## Game hooks (raw material for the Fun scout, not decisions yet)
| Trait | Possible use |
|---|---|
| Ball | The main thing to chase or fetch |
| Cheese | Bonus pickup or power-up |
| Burrowing into blankets | Hiding or safe spots, or a tunnel move through blanket piles |
| Zoomies | A speed boost or frenzy mode |
| Stealing things | Grabbing items (socks, shoes) for points |
| Odd sleeping poses | Game-over or idle animation, e.g. flopping upside down |
| Other dogs | Hazards to avoid |
| Baths | A hazard such as a tub, bubbles, or a hose spray |
| Feathery ears and plumed tail | Juice: ear flap, tail wag, squash and stretch |

## Originality guardrail
Zoe is drawn procedurally and in an original style. Don't borrow from existing cartoon dogs, mascots, or game art.
